── Part 4: Attack — Tamper the Device Identity ──

    Scenario: Attacker changes the idhash to claim a DIFFERENT TPM produced this proof

    Original idhash (from proof):  891421482732113297
    Tampered idhash (attacker):    999999999

    zkp-prover --verify --proof=<proof> --clat=130330000 --clon=176229300 --radius=100000 --idhash=999999999

    ❌  Proof INVALID: public inputs mismatch

    Why it failed:
      The proof was generated with idhash = 891421482732113297 (SHA-256 of TPM AK pub)
      The attacker supplied idhash = 999999999
      The circuit rejects — the hash is part of the proof statement

    This means:
      • Proof is bound to a SPECIFIC TPM via SHA-256(AK pub)
      • geo-id-hash in cert = pre-committed binding (derivable from AK pub in cert)
      • You cannot transplant a valid proof to a different device
